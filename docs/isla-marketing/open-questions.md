# Isla Marketing Hub: Open Questions

Status: Phase 1. These are the questions we need Isla's owner to answer.
Last updated: 2026-11-20

How to answer: reply in any language and any format. Short answers are fine. Do not send passwords, access tokens or API secrets. They are never needed in documents or in GitHub.

## A. Essential questions (needed before Phase 2 and Phase 3)

### Business

1. What is the exact company name (legal name and brand spelling: Isla or ISLA)?
2. What does Isla offer today: which property types and which tourism services?
3. Which destinations are live today, and which is the priority for marketing? (The brand work lists Dahab, El Gouna, North Coast, with Cairo planned. Please confirm.)
4. What are the top three marketing goals for the next 6 to 12 months (for example more bookings, more inquiries, more followers), with numbers if possible?
5. Who are the main audiences, and in which languages and countries?
6. Who will use the Hub, and what should each person be allowed to do? Please give names or roles.
7. Who approves content before it is published?
8. How do inquiries and bookings reach Isla today (website form, WhatsApp, phone, direct messages, booking platforms)? Where are they recorded?

### Social media accounts

9. Please confirm the Facebook Page (facebook.com/IslaEgypt) and TikTok account (@isla.egypt). What is the Instagram account name, if one exists?
10. Are there other channels the Hub should include later (YouTube, WhatsApp Business, Google Business Profile, others)?
11. Who owns and administers each account today? Are any of them registered under personal profiles instead of company profiles?
12. Is the Instagram account a Professional (Business or Creator) account, and is it linked to the Facebook Page?
13. Who has the authority and the login access to approve the connections for each platform? This person will need to log in once per platform during Phase 3.

### Technical and legal

14. Does Isla have a public website? If yes, what is the address, and does it show a Privacy Policy and Terms of Service? Both TikTok and Meta need these.
15. Does Isla already have a Meta Business account (business portfolio), and is it verified?
16. Which hosting approach is preferred, and what is the monthly budget range?
17. Are there rules about where data must be stored (for example inside Egypt)?
18. Who is Isla's technical contact for domain name and website settings?
19. Should the Hub stay independent, or later become the Marketing Management part of Isla Business OS?
